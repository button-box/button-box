# Maintainers

The project is currently maintained by `danpeg`, with `edsammy` credited for
the substantial installation, configuration, contact-routing, NFC, and
onboarding work promoted into this candidate.

Before the first public release, document:

- who can merge and publish releases;
- how a maintainer is added or removed;
- the minimum review required for security, privacy, hardware, and dependency
  changes;
- who owns private vulnerability reports and release signing.

No single automated check can authorize a physical deployment or public
release. Those decisions require a named human reviewer and recorded evidence.
