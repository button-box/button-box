## Button Box v0.3.9

### New
- **The box says what you just sent.** After a reaction card send is accepted, the box plays the card sound and then says "You just sent a heart!" (or a smile, a kiss, applause, and so on) in the current voice. Every voice pack has the line for the twelve common reactions, and any other reaction says "You just sent a reaction!". The line follows the "Say the name when a card is tapped" setting. A send that fails plays only the "oops" sound and never claims a send.
- **Family cards can come pre-connected in a kit.** On a Cloud box, family cards packed with a kit and accepted by the box's owner pair to their connected box automatically. The box never moves or removes a card that was paired on the box, and a card a parent removed is not paired again until it is paired on the box.

### Fixed
- Updates no longer fail when a timer-started service is briefly starting at the moment the update checks the box.
- An attended update can now run while a box is still in setup mode, and rolls back to setup mode exactly.
- Rollback is more reliable when a watcher service had reached its restart limit before the update.

These updater fixes are in the fixed update controller. A signed update cannot replace that controller; boxes that already have the updater receive these fixes through attended maintenance.
