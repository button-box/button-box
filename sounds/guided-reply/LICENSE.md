# Guided-reply audio assets

These five prompt recordings are distributed under a separate audio-asset
license held by the project owner. Redistribution permission for inclusion in
the Button Box repository and its releases was confirmed on 2026-08-24. The
underlying license record is retained privately; these assets are not licensed
under the repository's source-code license and must not be reused separately
unless the asset license permits it.

The prompts use the macOS Samantha voice at 165 words per minute. They were
rendered offline, normalized to -18 LUFS, and converted to 48 kHz, 16-bit mono
PCM WAV files.
